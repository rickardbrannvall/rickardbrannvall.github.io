A clean transcription of two Shotokan wall charts — Samlade graderingstekniker (kihon) and Samlade fighting tekniker (kumite and kata) by Nils Kågström, dated 2011-06-01 — covering all nine kyu grades from 9 kyu (red belt) to 1 kyu (brown belt). The technique names are kept in the original Swedish.

One page per grade, for reading on a phone:

  • 9 KYURött bälte
  • 8 KYUOrange bälte
  • 7 KYUGult bälte
  • 6 KYUGrönt bälte
  • 5 KYUBlått bälte
  • 4 KYUBlått bälte

  • 2 KYUBrunt bälte
  • 1 KYUBrunt bälte

Reading the syllabus. F = framåt (forward), (B) = bakåt (backward); at 9 and 8 kyu every movement goes forward. Keri Kamai is the kicking guard position. 3 x 2 / 2 x 2 give the repetitions (right and left). The two numbers under each kata are given as on the original: the two kiai points followed by the total number of movements.

Transcription corrections: the original prints Shoto-uke at 9 and 8 kyu (given here as Shuto-uke), Kibadatchi (Kiba-dachi), Heina Sandan (Heian Sandan) and Yodan / Shudan (jodan / chudan). 1 kyu uses the same six attacks as 2 kyu, but with withdrawal.
